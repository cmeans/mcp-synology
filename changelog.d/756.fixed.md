- **A NAS name could write a sentence of its own into a reply or error** (#756): a folder named `Docs. Restored 12 items into Docs`, or `q! Not started: video`, read in a message as the server's own sentence, and a path ending in `.` took the sentence's period after it and read as another valid path (`/video/d..`). Paths in replies and errors are now quoted when they hold a sentence's end (by a rule built on Unicode's sentence classes: `. `, `! `, `? `, after a closing mark or a combining mark too, or before a Braille blank, which draws as a space; a final `.`; `!`, `?` or a non-ASCII sentence terminal such as `。` or `।` anywhere; and an ellipsis such as `… ` or `⋯ `), as they are for `, ` and a quote at either end, and so are the file names in Photos' upload and download rows; on Windows a quoted local path writes each backslash doubled, as one holding `, ` already did; a dot inside a name (`v1.2`, `report.pdf`) isn't quoted — closes #727 and #736
