- **A NAS name could write a sentence of its own into a reply or error** (#756): a folder named `Docs. Restored 12 items into Docs`, or `q! Not started: video`, read in a message as the server's own sentence, and a path ending in `.` took the sentence's period after it and read as another valid path (`/video/d..`). Paths in replies and errors are now quoted when they hold a sentence's end (Unicode's: `. `, `! `, `? `, after a closing mark too, a final `.`, a CJK full stop), as they already were for `, ` and a quote at either end; a dot inside a name (`v1.2`, `report.pdf`) isn't quoted — closes #727 and #736
